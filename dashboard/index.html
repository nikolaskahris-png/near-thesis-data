Investment Thesis · NEAR HACKATHON

NEAR's value capture is real, small and discretionary.

Users pay real fees on NEAR Intents, but only a small part reaches NEAR holders, and every link from the fee a user pays to the NEAR a holder keeps can be changed by a few signers or by a company off-chain; none is fixed in code. Rating: Hold, not Buy, to 2031. Hold: keep NEAR you own, staked. Upgrade to Buy only once (b) keeping it is a rule rather than a choice, and then when (a) what the buybacks wallets keep grows fast enough to cover issuance by 2031, in dollars and in NEAR, or what they keep already covers all new NEAR. Downgrade to Avoid if, twice in a row, the wallets keep less than three quarters of DefiLlama's revenue, or what the buybacks wallets keep in dollars falls back to April's level. Avoid overrides Buy; Hold has no upper price.
