NEAR's value capture is real, small and discretionary.

For each dollar of new NEAR issued, the buybacks keep NEAR worth about ( of issuance), and burn none of it. The NEAR bought sits in two wallets that signers can move; the fees users pay are set off-chain. Only issuance is a protocol rule, changed only by a release run by 4/5 of stake.

Rating, latest window: —

The README's rules applied to a rolling window of thirty days, every day (data/rating.csv). Avoid needs the latest window and the one ending thirty days before it to fail one of the two tests, or the bought NEAR to fall more than a tenth below its peak; Buy needs rule (b) on chain first: a lock no one can spend from. A window with missing DefiLlama days is not judged, and the two newest windows may still be revised.

  • Buy only when (b) the buybacks sit in a declared lock the chain shows cannot send NEAR out (no keys and no code, or reviewed code and no full-access key) and treasury.near sent nothing in the window, and either the NEAR they keep, counted up to DefiLlama's revenue and fees, covers all new NEAR, or (a) it grows a year, in dollars and in NEAR.
  • Avoid when this thirty-day window and the one ending thirty days before it each fail a test: the wallets keep less than three quarters of DefiLlama's revenue, or the buybacks wallets keep April's a day or less (at this window's NEAR kept, a price of or less), unless DefiLlama's fees fell too and the Confidential Intents Treasury account's stablecoins grew. Also Avoid, at once, while the buybacks stock is more than a tenth below its highest since the first of November. Avoid overrides Buy; Hold has no upper price.
