Investment Thesis · NEAR HACKATHON

NEAR Intents' buybacks are a policy, not a rule, and this page checks them every day.

NEAR Intents is a real business: users paid $206,049 a day in fees in October (DefiLlama), and part of that buys NEAR. On chain, the buyback wallets kept NEAR worth 11 cents for every dollar of new NEAR issued that month.

So far they have kept it: 96.9% of NEAR Intents' revenue since April stayed in its wallets. But nothing makes them: one or two signers can move each wallet's NEAR (the buybacks wallets, 2 of 4), and none of it is burned or locked. Every other money lever we mapped on chain also moves with one to three signers; only issuance needs validators with 4/5 of the stake.

Rating to 2031: Hold. Stay staked: stakers receive the new NEAR, minus the treasury's 10.1% and their validator's commission; unstaked NEAR receives none. Don't add: what reaches holders is small, and no rule fixes it.

  • Avoid, at once, if the NEAR in the buyback wallets ends a day more than a tenth below its peak since the first of November (moving it to staking counts as a fall). The test below runs live, from your browser.
  • Buy needs a lock the chain shows sealed (we tested that check on testnet), a treasury with no new payment requests in the window, and buybacks that cover all new NEAR — ×9.2 what they keep today — or grow by +75% a year.

Next dated checks: 16 November, when HSP-029 leaves its timelock (2026-11-16 16:48 UTC); it cuts issuance only once validators with 4/5 of stake run a release. Then 9 December, when a prediction on what the buybacks keep is scored.

Rating, latest window: —

The README's rules applied to a rolling window of thirty days, every day (data/rating.csv). Avoid needs the latest window and the one ending thirty days before it to fail one of the two tests, or the bought NEAR to fall more than a tenth below its peak; Buy needs rule (b) on chain first: a lock no one can spend from. A window with missing DefiLlama days is not judged, and the two newest windows may still be revised.

  • Buy only when (b) a published rule sends the buybacks to a declared lock the chain shows cannot send NEAR out (no keys and no code, or reviewed code, no full-access key and only function-call keys limited to the lock) and treasury.near sent nothing in the window, and either the NEAR they keep, counted up to DefiLlama's revenue and fees, covers all new NEAR, or (a) it grows +75% a year, in dollars and in NEAR.
  • Avoid when this thirty-day window and the one ending thirty days before it each fail a test: the wallets keep less than three quarters of DefiLlama's revenue, or the buybacks wallets keep April's $6,270 a day or less (at this window's NEAR kept, a price of $0.655 or less), unless DefiLlama's fees fell too and the Confidential Intents Treasury account's stablecoins grew. Also Avoid, at once, while the buybacks stock is more than a tenth below its highest since the first of November. Avoid overrides Buy; Hold has no upper price.

Also measured: NEAR Intents moved $4.02B in DefiLlama's thirty days to 7 November (not the window) · a staked holder's share of all NEAR grows 2.44% a year before the validator's commission, an unstaked holder's changes -2.40%; to 2031, after an assumed validator commission and with HSP-029, a staked holder's share grows 6.3% · since the flow went private the three revenue wallets kept 13,800 NEAR a day, against 14,261 before, while DefiLlama's revenue changed -6% in NEAR · the Confidential Intents Treasury account held $123.5M at the end of that comparison.
